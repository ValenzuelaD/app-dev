# My Favorite Series
## Emily in Paris
*Emily in Paris* is one of my favorite Netflix series. It follows the life of **Emily Cooper**, a marketing professional who moves to Paris for work.

Why I Like *Emily in Paris*
- Beautiful Paris scenery
- Stylish fashion
- Interesting characters
- Fun and engaging story

Main Characters
1. **Emily Cooper**
2. Gabriel
3. Mindy Chen
4. Camille
5. Sylvie

Favorite Quote
> You can never make everyone happy. You are not a jar of Nutella.

Genre: Romantic Comedy, Drama

Platform: Netflix
